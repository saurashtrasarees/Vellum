Decade of Weaving Indian Elegance & Mill Quality

With over a decade of continuous manufacturing in the world-renowned textile capital of Surat, Saurashtra Sarees has stood as a bastion of authentic Indian saree manufacturing. We have evolved into a centralized, modern manufacturing and super-stockist power plant.

Every collection is developed in-house—from selecting high-denier warp yarns and fine mulberry and art-silk fibers to engineering intricate jacquard punch patterns. Our master weavers blend centuries of Banarasi, Kanjivaram, and Paithani traditions with cutting-edge precision to deliver sarees celebrated for rich drape, radiant sheen, and lasting durability.
