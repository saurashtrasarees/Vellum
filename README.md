## 🌟 Overview

**Vellum** replaces bulky PDF catalogs and messy Google Drive links with a **Shopify-grade interactive web showroom**. Built specifically for B2B wholesale and portal seller networks, it delivers a frictionless, visual-first lookbook experience with zero checkout clutter.

---

## ✨ Core Pillars & Features

* **👗 Dual-Shoot Perspective Engine:**
  * **Model Shoot:** High-fashion lifestyle draping, angle shots, and reel clips.
  * **Table Shoot:** Authentic flat-lay captures showing true pallu weave, zari sheen, border finish, and fabric texture.
* **🔗 Smart Link Tiered Pricing (Zero Effort for Buyers):**
  * Dynamic rate cards unlocked automatically via shareable links:
    * `?tier=wholesale` ➔ Wholesale & Super Stockist rates
    * `?tier=portal` ➔ Reseller & Portal Seller rates
    * `?tier=retail` ➔ Catalog MRP / Retail display
  * No buyer logins, passwords, or app installations required.
* **⚡ Pure Visual Showcase:**
  * Clean, fast, mobile-first lookbook without cart, checkout, or payment gateway barriers.
* **💬 1-Click WhatsApp & Call Booking:**
  * Instant inquiry buttons pre-filled with catalog name, design code, color swatch, and active tier rate:
    * 📞 `+91 9879471039`
    * 📞 `+91 9913643433`
* **📁 Drive-Native Ingestion:**
  * Catalogs, fabric specs, and rates sync directly from Google Drive `.txt` files and media directories.
